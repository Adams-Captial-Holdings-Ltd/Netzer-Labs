# Brand

## Company

**Netzer Labs**

## Product

**Netzar OS Enterprise**

Short form "Netzar OS" is acceptable in running copy once the full name has been
used. `EnterpriseOS` survives only as the npm package name (`enterpriseos`), not
as a product name.

## Repository

`emmanuelmadams4-lgtm/Netzer-Labs`

## Positioning

Netzer Labs helps companies build, connect, govern, and operate AI networks they control. Netzar OS Enterprise provides the agent runtime, knowledge layer, integrations, governance controls, and customer-owned AWS infrastructure for that network.

## Brand idea

“Netzer” means “branch” in Hebrew. The name reflects durable systems that grow from a strong root: practical AI infrastructure, connected intelligence, and long-term enterprise value rather than short-lived hype.

## Legal status

Netzer Labs is the operating brand selected by Emmanuel Adams for this repository. Complete formal corporate-name, trademark, and domain clearance before public launch, customer contracting, or trademark filing.

## Compatibility

Historical `claudeclaw` and `CCE` identifiers remain in selected internal AWS resource names, environment variables, and migration contracts. They are compatibility identifiers, not the public company or product brand. Renaming them requires a separately tested migration.
